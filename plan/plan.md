# Copy Trim — Hero, Services, How We Work

KNDP is a Greek digital agency whose homepage sells the studio's development services to local businesses. The current copy in three sections repeats ideas, explains what the visuals already show, and adds words that slow the visitor down without adding meaning.

**Who it's for:** Visitors landing on the homepage who scan rather than read — the goal is to let the design carry the weight and reduce copy to the minimum that still converts.

---

## What changes, section by section

### Hero
**Current subheading (2 lines):**
> "Από ιστοσελίδες και εφαρμογές έως έξυπνα εργαλεία και αυτοματισμούς, χτίζουμε τις ψηφιακές λύσεις που η επιχείρησή σου χρειάζεται για να αναπτυχθεί."

**Trimmed to 1 short line:**
> "Websites, apps, εργαλεία, αυτοματισμοί — ό,τι χρειαστείς."

**Floating tooltip on the ChatDesktop widget** — currently has a label *and* an explanation sentence. The explanation ("Γράψε την επιχείρησή σου και δες τι μπορούμε να χτίσουμε για σένα.") is removed. The label "Δοκίμασέ το τώρα" stays — it is enough.

---

### Services
Three cuts:

1. **Section intro label** removed — "Η πλήρης λίστα — ομαδοποιημένη με βάση τι σημαίνει για εσένα" adds nothing the visual grouping does not already show.

2. **The `who` line on every card** removed — the small uppercase "Για επιχειρήσεις που…" line at the bottom of each card repeats what the card body already implies. Removing it tightens each card.

3. **CTA card body text** trimmed from 2 sentences to 1:
   > "Πες μας το πρόβλημα — θα σου πούμε ειλικρινά τι χρειάζεται."

---

### How We Work
One cut:

- **Tagline below the CTA button** removed — "Δωρεάν σχέδιο εντός 48 ωρών — χωρίς δέσμευση." repeats step 02 which already says the same thing. The button label covers this on its own.

---

## What stays exactly as-is
- All headlines and subheadings
- All section structures and layouts
- All other sections of the site
- Step descriptions in How We Work (already compact)

---

## Assumptions
- "Websites, apps, εργαλεία, αυτοματισμοί — ό,τι χρειαστείς." is an acceptable shorter subheading — the exact wording can be adjusted after seeing it live.
- Removing the `who` line from service cards is purely a cut with no replacement text.
- No layout changes are made — only text nodes are removed or replaced.
