# Hero Headline Slogan Swap

The hero section currently displays "Χτίζουμε / Ό,τι / Χρειάζεσαι." across three animated lines. The new slogan agreed on is the Greek equivalent of "We build anything, literally, anything."

**Who it's for:** Visitors landing on the KNDP homepage — the headline is the first thing they read.

## Change

Replace the three-line hero headline with:

> Line 1: "Χτίζουμε τα πάντα."
> Line 2: "Κυριολεκτικά,"
> Line 3: "τα πάντα."

The accent highlight (baby-blue colour) currently on "Ό,τι" moves to "τα πάντα." on line 3 to land the emphasis on the repeated phrase — the part that makes the slogan memorable.

The subheading paragraph beneath the headline stays unchanged.

## Assumptions

- Greek translation used: "Χτίζουμε τα πάντα. Κυριολεκτικά, τα πάντα." (proposed in conversation and approved).
- Three lines kept so the existing WordMask stagger animation plays identically.
- Accent on "τα πάντα." (last line) rather than the middle line, since the repetition is the punchline.
- No other copy, layout, or animation changes.
